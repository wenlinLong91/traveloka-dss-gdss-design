# DSS and GDSS Design for Traveloka

## Project Overview

This project presents a consultant-style Decision Support System (DSS) and Group Decision Support System (GDSS) framework for Traveloka’s regional decision-making.

The proposed framework addresses the challenges of coordinating financial, workforce and operational decisions across multiple countries, service categories and time zones. It was developed from Traveloka’s publicly available business information and relevant academic literature rather than internal company data.

## Project Objectives

* Design a GDSS for structured cross-border collaboration.
* Propose a phased implementation and governance plan for a regional DSS.
* Identify DSS applications in finance and human resources.
* Evaluate privacy, fairness, transparency and accountability risks.
* Preserve human managerial judgement in data-supported decisions.

## Proposed Data Requirements

The DSS design identifies the following potential organizational data sources:

* Booking and revenue records
* Payment and profitability data
* Marketing campaign performance
* Customer-service data
* Workforce and payroll records
* Attendance and employee performance data
* Training and staff-development information

These data sources were identified as system requirements. No private or internal Traveloka data were accessed or analysed.

## Methods

The project used:

* Literature review
* Business case analysis
* Stakeholder and system requirements analysis
* Scenario planning
* Implementation-roadmap design
* Ethical and governance risk assessment

## GDSS Design

The proposed GDSS combines:

* Shared regional dashboards
* Video conferencing and enterprise messaging
* Shared documents and digital whiteboards
* Brainstorming and idea-categorisation tools
* Polling and voting functions
* Scenario analysis and consensus-building tools
* Digital records for accountability and follow-up

These components support synchronous and asynchronous participation by geographically distributed teams.

## DSS Applications

### Finance

The proposed finance DSS supports:

* Revenue and demand forecasting
* Budget allocation
* Regional profitability analysis
* Campaign-performance evaluation
* Pricing and promotion scenarios
* What-if analysis across markets

### Human Resources

The proposed HR DSS supports:

* Workforce planning
* Seasonal staffing forecasts
* Employee attrition analysis
* Training-needs identification
* Performance monitoring
* Cross-regional workforce allocation

## Governance and Ethical Considerations

The proposed governance framework includes:

* Role-based access control
* Data-quality monitoring
* Regular model review
* Privacy protection
* Bias and fairness assessment
* Transparent and interpretable recommendations
* Human-in-the-loop decision-making
* Cross-functional oversight involving legal, HR, data and operations teams

## Key Outcomes

The project produced:

* An integrated DSS and GDSS conceptual framework
* Finance and HR decision-support use cases
* A six-month implementation roadmap
* A stakeholder and governance structure
* Recommendations for privacy, fairness, transparency and human oversight

## Learning Outcomes

Through this project, I strengthened my skills in decision-support system design, business requirements analysis, cross-functional collaboration, implementation planning and responsible data governance. I also developed a clearer understanding of how DSS and GDSS technologies can support managerial judgement without replacing human decision-makers.

## Project Scope and Disclaimer

This is an independent academic case study based on public information. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or implemented at Traveloka. The framework is conceptual and does not describe Traveloka’s private internal systems.
